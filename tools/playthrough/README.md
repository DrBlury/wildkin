# Scripted playtime: integration prerequisites

`build/shot` accepts `wait`, `hold`, `tap`, `shot`, `rec`, `stop`, `save`, `peek` and `layer` commands. It **does not** accept warp-to-map, story flags, coordinates/waypoints, battle AI, or a total-frame counter. There are no executable per-act playthrough scripts yet: a `wait` script would measure idling, not travel, and a blind held-direction script cannot cross gates and fights. Do not cite an act duration from this harness until these capabilities and the remaining story/links/events/saga maps are integrated.

The C-side act XP/coins diagnostic in `act_balance.h` runs as part of `make test` (`tools/test_game.c`). It checks the integer team mean (not the starter alone) against ±2 and ≥Master−4 for the normal 6/3 wild-bout policy, plus gross coin affordability before spending/materials. Its separate zero-wild pass is a stricter stress diagnostic, not the no-grinding criterion. It assumes all map NPC wardens, all visible satchels, a spring/winter mature Glowberry plot and no purchases, healing or losses, so coins are a gross upper bound, not spendable cash guaranteed on the critical path. It does not count frames or prove route reachability. Once the harness can load a save with the previous act's flags and play a waypoint path using battle policy, record *actual emulated frames* from act start to Master victory, divide by 60 frames/s and 60 s/min, and show the raw minutes plus the heuristic 2.5× human estimate next to contract §10 targets (I 120 min; II–VII 120–150 each; VIII 60; critical path 960–1080). A human pass is needed before any claim of real playtime.
